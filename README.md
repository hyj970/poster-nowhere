A vintage travel poster for Nimbara, the city above the clouds — a floating island crowned by its glowing Beacon Tower, with a golden sunset, an airship on daily service, and waterfalls spilling into a sea of clouds. Rendered with headless Chromium and real fonts as a 1024×1024 PNG, drawn entirely in HTML/CSS/SVG.
